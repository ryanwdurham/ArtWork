The artist

Painting the world as it feels

Eric Engstrom is a landscape painter based in the Pacific Northwest. Working primarily in oils, he paints on location — in the field, beside rivers, on mountain trails — capturing light and atmosphere in real time.

His work has been collected by individuals across the United States. Each painting is a singular, unrepeatable moment — made by hand, in the presence of the actual landscape.

The artist

Eric Engstrom

I'm a landscape painter working in oils. I paint outside — on mountain trails, beside rivers, in open fields — because I believe the only way to truly capture a place is to be present in it.

Every painting begins as a direct response to light. I'm drawn to the transitional moments — dawn, dusk, the hour before a storm — when the landscape feels most alive.

I'm based in the Pacific Northwest, and the mountains, forests, and rivers here have shaped everything about how I see and paint.

Eric Engstrom

The work

How each painting is made

01

Painting on location

Every piece starts in the field. I hike to the site and paint directly from observation — responding to the real light, weather, and atmosphere of the place.

02

Oils on linen & canvas

I work primarily in oil on linen or canvas. Oils allow me to capture the depth, richness, and luminosity that landscape light demands.

03

One of a kind

Each original painting is singular — made in a specific moment in a specific place. Museum-quality prints are available for sold works.
